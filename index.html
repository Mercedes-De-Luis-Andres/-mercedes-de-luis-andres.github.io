Current and recent projects

SOCIAL SCIENCES | BoJoClub: Book Clubs in Journalism Culture
Austria · Vienna / Klagenfurt / International
Research and cultural project exploring reading communities, narrative journalism and memory through book-club methodologies, institutional collaborations and empirical pilot activities.
2024 · Research Collaborator
DIGITAL HUMANITIES | Vom Musikalisch-Schönen
Austria · Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW) / FWF
Research collaboration on Eduard Hanslick's criticism between aesthetics, journalism and scholarship, including transcription, TEI-XML encoding and linked open data.
2021 · Co-Researcher
JOURNALISM | Reporting Under Pressure
Sweden · DEMICOM, Mid Sweden University
North-South Europe research project examining working conditions and professional experiences of journalists during the COVID-19 pandemic.
2014 · Cultural Researcher, Journalist & Project Designer
JOURNALISM | Documenting Contemporary Danish Music
Denmark
Independent cultural research and fieldwork on contemporary Danish music, combining interviews, reporting, research and public dissemination.
2014 · Cultural Researcher & Journalist
JOURNALISM | Hans Christian Andersen in Spain & Travel Diaries
Denmark / Spain
Archival and cultural research connecting Hans Christian Andersen's intellectual and literary networks with Spain, developed through research, interviews and narrative journalism.
2008 · Radio Journalist & Producer
RADIO & PODCAST JOURNALISM | Current Affairs

Production and presentation of audio journalism addressing social, political and cultural affairs.
