Research & Projects

Current and recent projects

  • 2026 · Principal Investigator: SOCIAL SCIENCES — Book Clubs in Journalism Culture, Resilience & Crisis (Austria · University of Vienna). Research proposal developed and submitted within the WWTF Resilience & Crisis framework, bringing together an interdisciplinary network across social sciences, health, education and environmental research.
  • 2026 · Project Designer & Lead: HEALTH HUMANITIES — Applied Arts & Fiction (Austria · Klinikum Klagenfurt - KABEG). Designed and led a non-evaluative intervention combining reading, writing and art within a clinical setting. Project funded by KABEG.
  • 2023–2026 · Principal Investigator & Project Designer: SOCIAL SCIENCES — BoJoClub: Book Clubs in Journalism Culture (Austria · Vienna / Klagenfurt / International). Research and cultural project exploring reading practices, narrative journalism and memory through book-club methodologies, institutional collaborations and empirical pilot activities.
  • 2024 · Research Collaborator: DIGITAL HUMANITIES — Vom Musikalisch-Schönen (Austria · Austrian Academy of Sciences - ÖAW / FWF). Research collaboration on Eduard Hanslick's criticism between aesthetics, journalism and scholarship, including transcription, TEI-XML encoding and linked open data.
  • 2021 · Co-Researcher: JOURNALISM — Reporting Under Pressure (Sweden · DEMICOM, Mid Sweden University). Research project examining working conditions of journalists in Sweden, France and Spain during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • 2014 · Cultural Researcher, Journalist & Project Designer: JOURNALISM — Documenting Contemporary Danish Music (Denmark). Independent cultural research and fieldwork on contemporary Danish music, combining interviews and public dissemination.
  • 2014 · Cultural Researcher & Journalist: JOURNALISM — Hans Christian Andersen in Spain & Travel Diaries (Denmark / Spain). Archival and cultural research connecting Hans Christian Andersen's intellectual and literary networks with Spain, developed through research, interviews and narrative journalism.
  • 2008 · Radio Journalist & Producer: RADIO & PODCAST JOURNALISM — Current Affairs (Germany · Berlin). Co-production and presentation of audio journalism addressing social, political and cultural affairs.
